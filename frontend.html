The path to public information

Small steps. Informed citizens.

From first draft to final appeal, in four steps.

  1. Draft your request

    Say clearly what information you want, and from which department.

  2. Submit it

    Send it to the Public Information Officer with the application fee, by post or online.

  3. Wait up to 30 days

    A reply is normally due within 30 days of receipt. Some cases have different timelines.

  4. Appeal if needed

    No reply, or not satisfied? Check the first-appeal process and applicable deadlines.
